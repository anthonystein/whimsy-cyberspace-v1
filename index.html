01 / The condition

Good businesses often run on invisible effort.

A business can look functional from the outside while depending on memory, heroics, and repeated correction behind the scenes. The work gets done, but the process never gets stronger.

03 / Current implementation
Hidden Infrastructure 001

A live operating system for an independent retailer.

Primetime Paint & Paper is where the theory meets the counter, the phone, the inbox, the warehouse, the website, and the customer.

Primetime is the live environment. Whimsy Cyberspace is the architecture layer. Together, they expose what the business already knows, turn that knowledge into usable systems, and test whether the company becomes easier to operate without losing its humanity.
